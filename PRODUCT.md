# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Laravel 12, Blade, Vite and Tailwind CSS 4.

## Users

Inferred from the reference and the request: people responsible for purchasing cleaning, hygiene and maintenance supplies for companies and condominiums.

## Product Purpose

Inferred: make it easy to discover BPL's product categories and start a commercial conversation for recurring supply needs.

## Positioning

Not established by the repository. The home should communicate practical supply, dependable service and a curated range without inventing unsupported claims.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive on a mobile or desktop browser, scan product categories, learn which solutions fit their environment, and contact the commercial team.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The home is a responsive Blade page.
- Navigation, category anchors, CTAs and the contact form must work without a backend integration.
- Form submission is a local confirmation state only; no lead is transmitted by this task.
- Product names, pricing, testimonials and performance claims must not be fabricated.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the BPL name and the broad association with hygiene and day-to-day business or condominium supplies.
- Use the existing site only as reference; do not reproduce its layout or assets.

## Evidence on Hand

- Reference site: https://bplprodutos.com.br/
- Repository is a fresh Laravel starter with no product catalog data or approved brand asset pack.

## Product Principles

- Make the first action obvious.
- Let categories be understood at a glance.
- Use specific, useful language instead of unsupported superlatives.
- Make contact feel lightweight and human.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, labeled form controls, reduced-motion support and responsive layouts for small screens.

# BPL Home Design Direction

## World

Contemporary supply field manual: operational clarity, tactile paper surfaces and signal colors. The page should feel like a well-designed dispatch board for a business that keeps other spaces running.

## Palette

- Ink: `#11131B` for the hero, footer and high-contrast text.
- Ink soft: `#1C2030` for the process section.
- Paper: `#F7F5EF` for the catalogue surface.
- Signal: `#E9FF54` for primary actions and active markers.
- Electric: `#6E7BFF` for navigational emphasis and secondary highlights.
- Orange: `#FF7B45` for the contact close.

## Typography

- Space Grotesk for display, labels and navigation: compact, technical and warm.
- DM Sans for body copy and form input: readable at small sizes.
- Display headlines use tight tracking and short line lengths; body copy stays around a comfortable reading measure.

## Composition

- The first viewport pairs a decisive statement with a functional-looking supply diagram.
- Catalogue content is an asymmetric 12-column grid, with varied card widths and authored geometric product visuals.
- Sections alternate between dark operational surfaces and light paper surfaces to create pace.
- Controls are explicit, outlined and keyboard-visible; no decorative glass or generic gradient cards.

## Interaction

- Mobile navigation opens inline beneath the header.
- Category chips filter the catalogue without a page reload.
- Contact form confirms locally because no lead endpoint is established yet.
- Entrance reveals are restrained and disabled for reduced-motion users.

## Quality Bar

- The page must remain understandable and operable without animation.
- All navigation and form controls have semantic labels and visible focus.
- The home is useful at small widths, not just a scaled desktop composition.
